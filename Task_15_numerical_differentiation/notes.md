# Task 15 – Numerical Differentiation

## B. Inputs and Mathematical Operation
**Inputs:** Time and position measurements from GPS.  
**Operations:** Forward, central, and backward finite differences to estimate speed.

Result: The estimated speed around that time increased.

## D. Meaning of the Output + Graph
The calculated speeds show how fast the vehicle was moving at each time. The graph visualizes the speed profile.
